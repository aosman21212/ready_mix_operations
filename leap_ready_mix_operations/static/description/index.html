Key Features

🏭

Multi-Plant Management

Configure multiple concrete plants, each with its own silos, storage tanks, capacity, quality standard (EN 206 / ACI 318 / IS 456), and cost rates. Live fill-percentage and calibration status per silo.

🧪

Mix Design Library

Full concrete mix-design records with grade, target strength, slump limits, W/C ratio, cement content, aggregate size, and ingredient recipe lines. Mandatory approval and consultant sign-off workflow.

📍

Job Sites & Rate Agreements

Track customer job sites with GPS address, site contacts, and pump requirements. Lock agreed unit prices per site / plant / mix design with effective date ranges.

📅

Delivery Scheduling

Create call-off delivery schedules with requested volume and pour rate. One click auto-generates a sequenced dispatch plan (truck #, planned time, volume) based on fleet capacity.

🚛

Dispatch & Fleet

Assign transit mixers, drivers, and pump trucks to each dispatch. Full state lifecycle: Draft → Confirmed → Batching → In Transit → Discharging → Delivered. Direct link to Load Ticket creation.

📄

Load Tickets — Core Document

The heart of the system. Tracks batching time, departure, arrival, discharge start/end, drum revolutions, water added, volume ordered vs delivered, material consumption with moisture correction, and auto compliance-breach detection.

🔬

Fresh QC Tests

Record slump, air content, temperature, density, and W/C ratio directly against a load ticket. Automatic pass/fail per parameter compared to mix design limits. Failing tests auto-create an NCR.

🧊

Cube & Cylinder Specimens

Auto-generated 7-day and 28-day specimen records upon delivery. Record failure load and cross-section area; compressive strength is computed automatically. Failing specimens raise an NCR and can be certified to generate QC certificates.

⚠️

NCR / CAPA (8D)

Full non-conformance tracking with severity classification (Minor / Major / Critical), root cause analysis, immediate containment, corrective and preventive actions, and verification workflow.

💰

Cost Sheets & Margin

Per-load cost sheets covering material (computed from actual consumption), transport, driver, pump, and overhead — with one-click computation from plant rates. Revenue vs cost margin is tracked and locked on invoicing.

💧

Moisture Logs

Daily aggregate moisture log per plant and silo. Free moisture is auto-computed (moisture % − absorption %). Confirmed logs feed into batch-level moisture correction on load material lines.

📜

Certificate Register

Color-coded tracking for plant, personnel, and vehicle certificates. Status auto-computed as Valid / Expiring Soon (≤30 days) / Expired. Dedicated view for expiring and expired certificates.

♻️

Washout Records

Environmental compliance records for each truck washout: returned concrete volume, washout water used, disposal method, and operator sign-off.

🧾

Invoicing Wizard

Group un-invoiced delivered loads by Job Site, Sales Order, Day, Mix Design, or create a single invoice. Generates Odoo invoices with one line per load ticket and marks them as invoiced automatically.

📊

Sales Order Integration

Extends the standard Odoo Sales Order with job site, plant, rate agreement, and technical review fields. Smart buttons show linked Schedules and Load Tickets with delivered volume.

🖨️

PDF Load Ticket Report

Print-ready delivery ticket with full material consumption table, timeline, moisture corrections, compliance status, and site signature block.

Screenshots

Operations Dashboard — Load Ticket List

At-a-glance KPI strip (loads today, volume, fleet utilisation, QC pass rate, open NCRs) above a colour-coded load list with live status badges and invoiced indicators.

Load Ticket Form

The core operational document: parties, fleet, volume, pricing, full timeline with computed durations, material consumption with moisture correction, and real-time compliance breach detection.

Quality Control — Tests, Specimens, NCRs & Certificates

Fresh test results with automatic pass/fail per slump and air content; specimen compressive-strength results; 8D NCR lifecycle; and the certificate register with expiry colour coding.
